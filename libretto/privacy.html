Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Libretto is built so that your prompts stay yours. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking of what you write. This page explains exactly what the app does with your data.

Summary

  • Your prompts, folders and tags are stored on your device and sync through your own private iCloud.
  • Newton Labs has no server that stores your prompts and cannot read them.
  • The Libretto keyboard works without Full Access. It never collects what you type.
  • Prompt Cleanup runs on your iPhone with Apple Intelligence.
  • Your prompt is sent to an AI app only when you choose to open it there.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in Libretto (prompts, titles, notes, folders, tags, version history and your settings) is stored on your device. Libretto does not require an account, and Newton Labs does not receive this content.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, Libretto syncs your library between your devices using Apple’s iCloud (CloudKit) in your private database. This data belongs to your Apple ID and is handled under Apple’s terms and privacy policy. Newton Labs cannot access it. You can stop syncing by turning off iCloud for Libretto in your iOS settings, or by signing out of iCloud, at any time.

The Libretto keyboard and Full Access

The keyboard shows your prompts and inserts the one you pick. It works without Full Access. If you choose to turn Full Access on, the keyboard can note which of your own prompts you inserted, so your Recent list and Used counts stay up to date. That information stays on your device in storage shared between Libretto and its keyboard. Libretto never collects, stores or transmits what you type, and the keyboard does not send anything off your device.

iOS switches to its own keyboard in password and other secure fields, so Libretto cannot see those.

Sending a prompt to an AI app

When you tap to open a prompt in an AI app (for example Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, Perplexity, Gemini or Copilot), Libretto copies the prompt to your clipboard and, where the app supports it, opens the app with the text filled in. From that moment the prompt is handled by the app you chose, under that app’s own privacy policy. Libretto does not send your prompts to any service of its own. Messages, Notes and Mail open with the text you chose in the same way.

Prompt Cleanup (Pro)

Prompt Cleanup runs on your device using Apple Intelligence. The text you clean is processed on your iPhone and is not sent to Newton Labs. Apple describes how Apple Intelligence handles data on its own pages.

Widgets

Widgets show prompt titles only. They are built on your device from your library and are not sent anywhere.

Information we collect

Libretto does not collect personal information, and it does not include third-party advertising, tracking or analytics. Newton Labs does not receive your name, email address, Apple ID, location, contacts, photos or prompts through the app.

What we do not collect

  • No names, email addresses or Apple ID information
  • No prompt text, titles, notes, folders or tags
  • No keystrokes or anything you type in other apps
  • No location data
  • No advertising identifiers (IDFA)
  • No payment information. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple.

In-app purchases

Libretto offers a one-time purchase (Libretto Pro) that unlocks extra features. All payment processing is handled by Apple. Newton Labs never receives, stores or processes your payment details.

Crash reports from Apple

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iOS settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash and usage reports for Libretto. They do not identify you and do not contain your prompts.

If you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and we use it only to reply to you. The “Contact support” button in the app prepares an email in your own mail app. It adds only the app version, iOS version, device model and the system’s error text, never your prompts, and nothing is sent until you send it.

Children’s privacy

Libretto is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
