How it works

From a good prompt to a one-tap habit.

1
Save it once

Write a prompt, or start from a ready-made one. Anything you create without a folder waits in the Inbox.

2
Sort it your way

Put it in a folder, add tags, pin the ones you use most. Search finds the rest.

3
Fill in the blanks

Mark what changes with {{variables}}. Libretto asks for each one, then shows a preview before you launch.

4
Send or insert

Open it in your AI app, or insert it from the Libretto keyboard in almost any app. It is always copied first.

Features

Everything for the prompts you reuse.

📌
Pinned and Recent

Pin the prompts you use every day and they stay at the top. Recent shows what you used last, so the right one is always a tap away.

🗂️
Folders and tags

File each prompt in one folder and add as many tags as you like. Search across everything, or filter by tag.

📥
Inbox

Capture a prompt now, sort it later. New prompts wait in the Inbox until you have a minute to file them.

🧩
Templates with variables

Wrap the part that changes in double curly braces, like {{topic}}. Libretto asks you to fill it in, shows a preview, then launches.

🚀
Send to your AI app

Open a prompt in Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, Perplexity, Gemini or Copilot. It is copied first, every time, and filled in where the app supports it. Set a default, or choose each time.

⌨️
A free keyboard

Insert a saved prompt into Mail, Messages, Notes and most chat apps without leaving the app you are in. The keyboard is free.

✨
Starter prompts

Ready-made prompts for everyday jobs. Hide the ones you don't need, or duplicate one to make it yours.

🎨
Make it yours

List, Compact or Sticky notes view, with pastel note colors, folder colors and Library backgrounds like cork and chalkboard.

☁️
Private iCloud sync

Your prompts follow you to your other devices through your own iCloud, and come back if you reinstall. No Libretto account.

🔎
Fast search

Find a prompt by its title, its text or its tags. Swipe to pin or delete, press and hold for more, and drag prompts between folders.

🕘
Version history

With Pro, Libretto keeps the last 25 versions of each prompt. See what changed, then copy or restore an earlier one.

🔒
Private by design

No account, no ads, and no tracking of what you write. Your prompts stay on your devices and in your own iCloud.

We never see your prompts.

Libretto has no account and no ads. Your prompts live on your device and sync through your own private iCloud. They are only sent to an AI app when you tap to open one.
