Support

Questions, feedback, or something not working as expected? We’re here to help.

✉️
Email support

Send us a message and we’ll get back to you as quickly as we can. Include your iOS version and the Libretto version (Settings > About > Version) to help us troubleshoot faster. Please don’t send us your prompts unless you want us to look at one.

Keyboard not showing your prompts?

Open Libretto once so it can share your prompts with the keyboard, and check that Libretto is switched on in iOS Settings > General > Keyboard > Keyboards.

Privacy

Your prompts stay on your device and in your own iCloud, and Libretto never collects what you type. Read the full Privacy Policy.
