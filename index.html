Every decision
intentional.

Newton Labs builds small, focused tools for iPhone and Apple Watch. One person, start to finish. Software that does what it promises — and nothing it doesn't.

Native First

Built in Swift and SwiftUI, for iOS from the first line. No cross-platform shortcuts. No web wrappers. Apps that feel at home on day one and improve with every system release.

Privacy by Default

Your data lives on your device, or in your own iCloud. No tracking SDKs. No analytics phoning home. No ad networks. What's yours stays yours.

Thoughtful Design

Every screen has to earn its place. The aim is software that feels obvious after you've used it — the kind you stop noticing.

Made to be Used

Each app starts with a problem that showed up in real life. If it wouldn't get opened at the end of a long day, it doesn't ship.

A studio of one is a choice. There is no committee to please and no roadmap to perform. When something is broken, it gets fixed. When an idea is worth building, it gets built. The apps stay small. The decisions stay clean.
