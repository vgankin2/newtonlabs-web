Restore a purchase

If you've already purchased TabCut Pro and it's not showing as active, open TabCut, go to Settings, and tap Restore Purchase. Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Privacy

TabCut processes receipt photos entirely on your device — nothing is uploaded to any server. Your split data lives in your personal iCloud account and is never accessible to us. Read our full Privacy Policy.
